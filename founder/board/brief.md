# Board Brief: Esküvői Újság (Wedding Gazette)

## The Idea

A self-service web application where engaged couples create, customize, and print professional wedding magazines, invitations, and save-the-date cards in minutes with live WYSIWYG preview.

**Target:** Engaged couples in Hungary (25–45, tech-savvy), planning weddings.

**What it sells:**
- Digital version (watermark-free PDF): 5,000 HUF (~€17)
- Printed copies: 500 HUF/copy (min. order 10)

## Current State

- **Product:** Live and in use (v6.47)
- **Tech:** Single-file web app (HTML/CSS/JS), Firebase backend for storage and auth
- **Features:**
  - Wizard: product type (magazine, invitation, save-the-date) + style pack selection (Romantic, Modern, Magazine, Wedding Magazine)
  - Editor: drag-and-drop layout, real-time preview, contenteditable text, image upload
  - Admin mode: add/remove sections, manage template structure
  - Printing: CSS print media for A4 + custom sizes
  - Languages: Hungarian + English
- **Monetization:** One-time purchase (no subscription)

## Market Context

- Target: Wedding planners and DIY-oriented engaged couples who want personalized, high-quality materials without hiring a designer
- Discovery channels: wedding blogs, Pinterest, Google ("esküvői újság"), word-of-mouth, wedding forums
- No paid marketing yet; no email system fully operational (SPF/DKIM not set up); no affiliate partnerships

## Constraints & Unknowns

- No significant customer acquisition spend
- Email authentication pending (blocks newsletters, reminders)
- Unclear: exact number of current users, repeat rate, average order value in practice
- No competitor analysis yet
- Pricing not validated against customer willingness-to-pay

## Founder Context

- Developer/solo founder (or co-founder), based in Hungary
- Can build, deploy, and maintain the app
- No marketing or sales team yet

---

The board will assess this business against three lenses: **Offers** (what would make it irresistible), **Monopoly** (defensibility and competitive advantage), and **Product** (design and execution).
