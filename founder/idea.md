# Esküvői Újság (Wedding Gazette)

- **What it is:** A self-service web application where engaged couples can create, customize, and print professional wedding magazines, invitations, and save-the-date cards in minutes with real-time preview.

- **Who it is for:** Engaged couples in Hungary (initially) planning weddings, aged 25–45, tech-savvy or willing to learn; particularly those who want personalized, high-quality printed materials without hiring a designer.

- **What it sells, at what price:**
  - Digital version (watermark-free PDF): 5,000 HUF (~€17)
  - Printed copies: 500 HUF per copy (minimum order 10 copies)
  - Bundled: e.g. 50 printed magazines of 4 pages = 10,000 HUF

- **Where and how:** Online (web app, no installation needed). Customers discover via wedding blogs, Pinterest, Google search ("esküvői újság"), word-of-mouth, and wedding forums. They sign up, use the wizard to pick a template and style, edit in a live WYSIWYG editor, then pay to download or order prints.

- **Budget and constraints:**
  - Hosted on Firebase (low-cost, scalable)
  - Built: already live and used (v6.47, single-file HTML/CSS/JS app)
  - Language: Hungarian + English
  - Revenue model: direct sales (no subscription, one-time purchase per project)
  - Constraints: no significant marketing spend yet; no email authentication fully set up (SPF/DKIM pending); no paid ads running.
